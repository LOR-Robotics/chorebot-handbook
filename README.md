# LOR Robotics handbook

How we develop and verify useful robots for supervised work on managed sites.

We begin in simulation, test on hardware under supervision, and evaluate a specific job with a site partner.
The work is in development. These stages do not imply certification or readiness for unattended use.

## Safety rule

Language models and operator apps request named, validated skills. They do not command wheel speeds.
Navigation, local motion protection and the physical emergency stop remain separate responsibilities.

## How we work

1. Understand the task and the people who do it.
2. Turn the need into a measurable requirement.
3. Design and implement the smallest useful change.
4. Test the behavior and retain evidence.
5. Review the result before release.

The [public wiki](https://github.com/LOR-Robotics/chorebot-handbook/wiki) is the public navigation point.
Reviewed page sources are in [wiki/](wiki/). Keep public copy generic; engineering plans and gate decisions
remain in private project records.
