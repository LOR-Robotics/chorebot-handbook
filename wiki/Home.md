# LOR Robotics handbook

We develop robots for useful, supervised work on managed sites. This handbook explains our working process.
The technology is in development; demonstrations are not claims of certification or unattended capability.

## Safety rule

Language models and operator apps request validated skills. They never command wheel speeds directly.
The local emergency stop and motion protection remain independent of conversational control.
